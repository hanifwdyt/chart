Fig. — Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an API key to start?

No. The free tier works with no key — just call the endpoint. Keys arrive with paid plans for higher limits and unmarked images.

Can I remove the watermark?

On the free tier, images are watermarked with chart.hanif.app. Paid plans render without it.

What are the rate limits?

The anonymous tier is rate-limited per IP, and identical requests are returned instantly from cache. Paid plans raise the per-key limits and add a priority render queue.

Which chart library is under the hood?

Chart.js v4, rendered server-side to a native canvas — so the config you write here matches the Chart.js docs you already know.

Can I self-host?

Yes, on the Business plan. The service ships as a container; reach out for a dedicated or on-prem setup.
